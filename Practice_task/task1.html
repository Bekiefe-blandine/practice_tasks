How to Cook Rice

Cooking rice is simple and easy. With a few ingredients, you can prepare delicious and fluffy rice.

Follow these simple steps to cook rice at home.

Ingredients

  • Rice
  • Water
  • Salt

Steps

  1. Wash the rice with clean water.
  2. Put the rice and water in a pot.
  3. Add a little salt.
  4. Boil the rice until the water is absorbed.
  5. Cover the pot and cook on low heat.
  6. Serve the rice when it is ready.
